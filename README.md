🌌 What is Kindnology?
Kindnology is not a project. It is a living universe where MEV hunters become gardeners, old blocks become artifacts, and blockchain history becomes shared memory.
We believe that extraction can transform into cultivation. Every address has a story worth preserving. Every transaction carries a seed.
Kindnology is an attempt to give blockchain a heart — through recognition, transformation, and shared history.
🌿 Philosophy
Proof of Kindness
A consensus of goodwill. We do not fight the dark forest — we illuminate it.
Proof of Contribution
A voluntary economic layer. Those who choose to contribute unlock premium access, private mempool routing, and shared upside. Those who do not are never blocked — they simply travel the public path, as free as ever.
The Three Principles
Creating good — every action should build, not destroy.
Generosity uplifts — value multiplies when shared.
We are the Garden — not a company, but a living organism.

🛡️ How It Works
Kindnology does not block, censor, or steal.
We operate a neutral RPC gateway with tiered service levels:
Standard Tier → Public mempool access via Flashbots Protect. Free. Open to all.
Premium Tier → Private mempool access, <10ms latency, priority inclusion. Unlocked via voluntary contribution to public goods funding.
